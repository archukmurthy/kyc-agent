# UBO Demo V1

## Purpose

UBO Demo V1 is an analyst-and-customer demonstration of existing UBO Lab research, ownership reasoning and Evidence capabilities. It is a review baseline for the demo experience, not a production release.

## Analyst flow

- Enter a UK company and run existing live Discovery or load a saved replay.
- Review the projected ownership, voting and control graph with relevant-company and full-map scopes.
- Inspect source assertions, registry context, calculation traces and contextual open work.
- Hand the current case, assertions and selected customer requests to the customer journey.

## Research and graph

Live research uses the existing recursive Companies House/Discovery composition and legacy-discovery adapter. Saved replay follows the same post-acquisition demo evaluation path without calling the provider. Demo-only automatic review makes safely sourced findings operative while unresolved facts remain visible to the existing planning flow.

The graph keeps economic ownership, voting rights and control distinct, preserves exact values and range endpoints, and supports relevant/full-map plus relationship filters. Browser refresh restores the case-bound demo result.

## Calculation methods

The demo presents recorded outputs from the existing effective-interest and control-attribution engines. Effective ownership multiplies each chain and adds independent direct and indirect interests. The Alice fixture demonstrates `10% + (60% × 30%) = 28%` with contributing paths and the engine's qualification explanation.

## Customer Evidence journey

The customer can upload an ownership chart through the existing Evidence extraction path. The journey presents certification separately, retains detailed candidate/source assertions and provenance, projects a chart-only ownership graph, calculates effective ownership where supported, and restores saved extracts without a repeated model call.

## Comparison behavior

The read-only comparison resolves cross-source parties before matching ownership assertions. Exact independent matches, values supported by independent ranges and overlapping independent ranges are distinguished from discrepancies and missing information. Original analyst/registry and customer-chart assertions remain unchanged and separately inspectable.

## Demo-only limitations

- UK Corporate 1.6-RC is a review/demo policy path.
- Browser-local demo state is not production persistence.
- Customer-uploaded facts remain candidate/source assertions until the normal decision process accepts them.
- Live results and demo auto-review are provisional and are not analyst-approved or production-authorised.
- Production authentication, deployment hardening, trusted-host work, customer Questions and Analyst Review are outside this release.
