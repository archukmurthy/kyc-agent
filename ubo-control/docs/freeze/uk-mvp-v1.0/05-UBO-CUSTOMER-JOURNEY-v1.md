# 05 — UBO Customer Journey v1

**Status:** CONTROL ROOM PRODUCT FREEZE  
**Important:** This is the applicant experience, not the UBO Lab

---

# 1. Experience principle

The applicant should feel:

> The system has already done the investigation. It shows what it knows and asks only for the few things still required.

The applicant must not see:

- the full analyst graph;
- all R01–R14 internals;
- raw candidate claims;
- every calculation diagnostic;
- one question per intermediary;
- provider failures presented as their problem.

---

# 2. Journey stages

1. Confirm company identity/profile.
2. System performs Discovery and existing-evidence resolution.
3. Planner chooses Discovery-led, chart-assisted or specialist route.
4. Show simple established-structure confirmation where useful.
5. Ask the minimum targeted facts/evidence.
6. Re-evaluate.
7. Present one residual-confirmation bundle where approved.
8. Customer UBO input completes.
9. Downstream KYC performs IDV/screening/POI/POA for projected people.
10. Internal/specialist review proceeds asynchronously where required.

---

# 3. Simple/data-rich UK company

System:

- identifies subject;
- obtains current direct ownership/control facts;
- calculates/attributes qualifying people;
- satisfies evidence rules.

Applicant sees:

> We found the following ownership and control information as of [date].

A small simple visual or list may show:

- direct owners;
- effective qualifying persons;
- qualifying basis in plain English.

Actions:

- Confirm;
- Something has changed.

Then a residual confirmation bundle if required.

No re-entry of known owners.

---

# 4. Partially resolved UK company

Example:

- ABC Holdings identified as owner;
- registry gives 25–50% band;
- exact value is decision-sensitive.

Applicant sees one targeted question:

> What percentage of [Customer] does ABC Holdings currently own?

Structured answer:

- exact;
- range;
- unknown.

If exact value must be corroborated, the same bundle requests the targeted evidence instead of creating a predictable second cycle.

---

# 5. Complex cross-border company with structure evidence

Where opacity is predictable, show:

> We cannot independently establish enough of this multi-layer ownership structure. Please provide a current ownership chart or equivalent structure evidence.

Accepted alternatives may include:

- chart;
- cap table;
- shareholder register;
- group-structure note;
- registry document;
- legal/accounting structure memo;
- jurisdiction-equivalent document.

Capture:

- as-at date;
- confirmer/provider identity;
- capacity.

After extraction:

> We verified 9 of 11 material relationships. We need 2 items.

Ask only for those 2.

---

# 6. Data-poor company without a chart

Offer a guided structure builder.

At each step collect:

- direct holder;
- entity/person type;
- jurisdiction;
- exact/range/unknown interest;
- currentness/as-at date.

Do not ask the applicant to calculate ultimate ownership.

Declared structure becomes candidate facts and is verified where possible.

---

# 7. “Something has changed”

This is a first-class route.

Applicant identifies:

- affected entity/relationship;
- changed holder/value/control fact;
- effective/as-at date;
- supporting evidence where appropriate.

The old claim remains historically preserved.

New input creates candidate facts/conflict or supersession workflow.

Re-run R09 comparison where relevant.

---

# 8. Control questions

Do not ask detailed control questions by default.

Use:

1. existing evidence/registry;
2. governance documents;
3. one residual confirmation bundle;
4. last-resort targeted structured customer question;
5. analyst review.

If a last-resort question is needed, ask concrete factual language, not legal conclusions.

Examples:

- Who holds the right?
- Which entity does the right concern?
- Is it current?
- Does it relate to a majority of the management body?
- What evidence records the right?

Do not ask “Who is the PSC?” or “Who has significant influence or control?” without factual explanation.

---

# 9. Residual confirmation bundle

One screen may contain separate statements:

- no undisclosed other control;
- no trust/similar arrangement;
- no nominee/bearer arrangement;
- no relevant joint arrangement;
- structure complete and accurate as of date.

Each statement is answered separately.

One positive answer opens only its own follow-up.

The exact wording and risk-tier sufficiency require sign-off.

---

# 10. Specialist journey

If the structure contains an unsupported trust, fund LP, foundation, sovereign/state structure, positive joint arrangement or ambiguous de facto control:

- stop ordinary recursive questions;
- collect only safe preparatory information;
- tell the applicant the ownership section has enough input for specialist review;
- allow the broader onboarding journey to continue according to host policy;
- do not expose internal legal complexity.

---

# 11. No UBO identified / fallback

After customer-resolvable work is complete:

- collect senior-management candidate information if missing;
- customer input completes;
- case moves to internal exhaustion review;
- customer does not wait synchronously.

If fallback is approved, downstream KYC verifies/screens the SMO.

If CDD cannot be completed, the host applies decline/termination/SAR-consideration policy.

---

# 12. Ownership visual for applicants

Optional, simple and calm.

Show only:

- established material ownership spine;
- qualifying people;
- one highlighted unresolved frontier where relevant.

Do not show:

- all control arrows;
- every diagnostic need;
- raw evidence IDs;
- analyst review controls.

Control can be shown as concise badges where material.

---

# 13. Confirmation provenance

Capture:

- confirmer identity;
- capacity;
- date/time;
- as-at date;
- source snapshot;
- affected entities/relationships;
- whether confirmed or changed.

Confirmation is evidence/provenance, not an automatic replacement for required independent proof.

---

# 14. Delegation

Allow a bundle to be delegated to an authorised knowledgeable person.

The delegate receives only the relevant task.

Capture capacity and provenance.

---

# 15. Customer interaction targets

Product benchmark:

- simple case: 1–2 interactions;
- partial case: 2–3 coherent interactions;
- complex/chart-assisted case: structure request plus one batched residual round;
- ASDA: approximately 2–4 coherent interactions, subject to actual evidence and sign-off.

These are benchmarks, not policy constants.

---

# 16. What the customer is never asked

Do not ask:

- every company's voting percentage;
- every intermediary's board-control facts;
- all minority owners where active thresholds cannot be met;
- a full structure already reliably established;
- provider-error remediation;
- internal review decisions;
- detailed UBO arithmetic;
- exact value where a band is sufficient for the active determination.

---

# 17. Completion distinction

`CUSTOMER_INPUT_COMPLETE` means no currently known customer-resolvable UBO work remains.

It does not necessarily mean:

- case resolved;
- analyst review complete;
- specialist review complete;
- application approved.

The host must distinguish customer completion from final UBO/case completion.
