# OwnershipGraphProjection v2 review exposure

`ubo-ownership-graph-projection-v2` accepts only a verified DecisionSnapshot v2 and is exposed for Wave 10 through `ubo-control/review`. It records canonical nodes, distinct economic/voting/control relationships, relationship currentness, evidence/support state, semantic node flags, calculations, qualification bases, causal InformationNeeds, dependent diagnostics, blockers, reviews, specialist routes and the exact pinned compatibility plan.

The reusable `OwnershipGraph` normalizes this contract for rendering only. It does not calculate ownership, infer qualifications or mutate the projection. Lab filters create a display copy of relationships for Ownership, Voting, Control or All; projection and snapshot hashes, qualification, needs and plan remain unchanged. Evidence status is shown separately from claim/currentness status.
